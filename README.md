# KyperX AI

**Australian AI engineering for practical organisational workflows.**

[KyperX AI](https://ai.kyperx.com/) helps organisations identify where AI is useful, automate defined workflows and build custom AI systems around their data, controls and operating requirements.

## What we do

- AI opportunity assessment
- AI workflow automation and AI agents
- Custom AI systems
- Systems integration and data pipelines
- Evaluation and production-readiness engineering
- Cloud, private-cloud, on-premises and hybrid AI infrastructure

## How we work

We begin with the operational workflow: its inputs, decisions, actions, exceptions and controls. We then determine where AI is appropriate, where deterministic software is a better fit and where human review should remain part of the system.

Our aim is not to maximise the amount of AI in a solution. It is to engineer a reliable system that fits the organisation's data, risk, integration and operating requirements.
